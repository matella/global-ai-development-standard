# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers of the Global AI Development Standard are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect the scope and purpose of the project, and the time of reviewers.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information.

Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block participants who repeatedly violate these expectations.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and select **Report a vulnerability**, and state that the report concerns conduct, not a security issue. Do not post personal information in public threads.

Reports are reviewed by the repository maintainers. Responses are made in good faith and kept as confidential as the situation allows.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
